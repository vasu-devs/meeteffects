Use this in a real Google Meet
  1. Open chrome://extensions (or brave://extensions) and enable Developer mode.
  2. Choose Load unpacked and select MeetEffects in Downloads.
  3. Reload Google Meet, turn on your camera, and open the MeetEffects pill.
  4. Disable Meet backgrounds. Try a meme in a test call and check it from a second device.

This studio is a local preview. Installing the extension connects the effects to Meet. Your Meet self-view may mirror text; the outgoing image is unmirrored.
