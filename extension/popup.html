MeetEffects

A little less meeting.
A little more cinema.

  1. Open or reload Google Meet.
  2. Turn your camera on.
  3. Open the MeetEffects pill at the top right.

Turn off Meet’s own backgrounds. Click Take room photo to save the current view, or use the 5-second timer to step out first. When the room is saved, come back and snap with your microphone on. Your movement stays live as you dissolve.

Keep the camera fixed to match your photo. Drag the MeetEffects button out of the way. Snaps use sound; claps and taps can trigger it too.

Real reaction images are built in. Enable Meme sounds in the Meet panel for short cues while your mic is unmuted.
